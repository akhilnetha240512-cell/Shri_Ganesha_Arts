Our Story

Made with Devotion, in Raikal Village

We are artisans from Raikal Village, Raikal Mandal, Jagtial District, Telangana. Every Bappa that leaves our workshop is shaped, dressed and decorated by hand — from the first coat of clay to the last crystal on the pagdi.

From tiny 1‑ft Selfie Bappas for your study table to grand 12‑ft Raja idols for the mandal pandal, we pour the same devotion into every size. Follow our journey and see new designs first on Instagram.
